# Keystone Percentile

**See where you rank in Mythic+:** your rating as "Top X%" overall, by role, by class and by spec, plus how your DPS ranks in every key you run.

## Rating percentiles

```
3,300  M+ rating, EU
Overall    Top 8.6%   ~#60,384 / 700,211
Healer     Top 7.9%   ~#13,415 / 169,016
Paladin    Top 8.2%   ~#5,775 / 70,000
Holy       Top 7.3%   ~#1,838 / 25,000
```

- Top % overall, by role, by class and by spec, for your region
- Your approximate ladder position in each group
- Your next bracket, Top 1% and Top 0.1%: rating needed and about how many keys
- Updates instantly after every key and when you change spec

## DPS in every key

- **End-of-key pop-up:** your DPS and your Top X% among Warcraft Logs players of your spec, in that dungeon, at that key level
- **See the gap:** a bar from 0 to the #1 run with the 50/25/10/1% marks, and how much more DPS each goal needs
- **Healers are ranked on HPS**, so every role gets a fair comparison
- **What happened:** deaths, avoidable damage taken and interrupts, compared with your own average
- **Your whole group** ranked against players of their own spec
- **Share** a run to group or guild chat with one click
- **Run history:** every key you've run, filterable by season, dungeon and spec, sortable by date, rank or DPS. Click any run to reopen its summary.
- **Your old logs too:** with the optional script, every run you've uploaded to Warcraft Logs is imported with its official rank

## Progress and goals

- **Rating graph:** your rating over the last 30 days or the whole season, with "this week: +42 rating · Top 9.4% → Top 8.6%"
- **Goals:** how much rating and about how many keys to your next bracket, Top 1% and Top 0.1%, with the exact keys to time when you want them
- **Exact brackets:** calculated from Raider.IO's full score distribution, not guessed

## Commands

Open everything from the minimap button, `/kp`, or Blizzard's Mythic+ window, which gets a Keystone Percentile panel on its side. `/kp goals`, `/kp runs` and `/kp progress` open those tabs, `/kp last` reopens the last summary, and `/kp config` opens the settings. An optional floating panel can be turned on with `/kp panel`.

Available in English, Deutsch, Français and Русский.

## Data

Fresh data is built into every release, daily. Rating data comes from the public [Raider.IO](https://raider.io) API, and DPS benchmarks from the [Warcraft Logs](https://www.warcraftlogs.com) API. Thanks to both. This addon isn't affiliated with Raider.IO, Warcraft Logs or Blizzard.

DPS benchmarks compare you with players who upload logs, who skew toward the more dedicated end, so "Top 50%" here is a higher bar than on the rating ladder. Hero talents aren't ranked by any public source yet.
